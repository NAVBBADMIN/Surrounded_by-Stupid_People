Foundations & the Red Profile

Before we can talk about anyone else's style, week one is about naming the loudest, fastest-moving one: Red. Direct, decisive, results-first — useful in a crunch, abrasive if it's the only gear in the box.

Briefing

What this week is about

Most behavioral-style models sort people into a handful of tendencies rather than fixed boxes — everyone is a blend, but most of us lean somewhere. Red is the style built for speed and results: quick to decide, comfortable with conflict, allergic to wasted time. In a crisis, Red gets things moving. In a discussion, Red can steamroll it.

This week's chapters lay the groundwork for the whole book: why these patterns exist, why they matter at work, and what Red looks like specifically. Read with one question in mind — where do I show up like this, and who on this team already does?
