The Yellow Profile

Yellow runs on people and possibility. This week is about spotting the energy that gets a room excited — and the follow-through it sometimes leaves behind.

Briefing

What this week is about

Yellow is the style that runs on people, ideas, and momentum. Enthusiastic, persuasive, quick to rally a room — and just as quick to lose the thread when the follow-through gets boring. Where Red asks "what's the decision," Yellow asks "who else is in on this."

These chapters cover what fuels Yellow, what drains it, and how it reads to everyone else in the room — especially the styles that find spontaneity exhausting.
