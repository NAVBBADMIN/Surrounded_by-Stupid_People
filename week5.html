CHAPTERS 18–21

Body Language, Perception & Stress

Nobody is one color under pressure. This week we look at how stress bends each style's body language and how our own lens distorts what we think we're seeing in someone else.

Briefing

What this week is about

Every style changes under pressure — and it usually shows in the body before it shows in words. This week's chapters connect the four styles to stress behavior and nonverbal cues: what it looks like when Red gets more forceful, Yellow gets more scattered, Green goes quiet, or Blue gets more rigid.

The second half is about perception — how we all judge other people's behavior through the lens of our own style, and how much of what we call "reading someone" is really just projecting.
